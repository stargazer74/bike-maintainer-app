digits.bin - digit model of the Bike Maintainer worksheet scan

Trained on the MNIST database of handwritten digits by Yann LeCun, Corinna Cortes and
Christopher J. C. Burges (http://yann.lecun.com/exdb/mnist/), licensed under CC BY-SA 3.0,
together with handwritten digits (c) 2026 Chris Wohlbrecht. Changes: weights of a neural
network trained on these digits with augmentation (affine jitter, stroke width, blur, noise,
European 1, 7 and 9).
